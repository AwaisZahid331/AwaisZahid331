### Hey, I'm Awais 👋

I'm a Full Stack AI Developer based in **Lahore, Pakistan**. I build things where LLMs actually do useful work — not just chatbots, but agents that reason, take action, and recover when something breaks.

I've spent the last **3+ years** shipping production AI systems across fintech, retail, education, and automation. Right now I'm an **Software Engineer at CBSOL**, where I design and deploy multi-agent workflows, RAG pipelines, and AI-integrated full-stack apps on AWS.

My stack lives at the intersection of **Python + FastAPI + LangChain** on the AI side and **React + Next.js + Node.js** on the product side. I care about systems that actually work in production, not just in demos.
